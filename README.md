## About

We at **Corvixa Labs** are excited to announce the development of our own web browser: **Corvixa Browser**.

Corvixa Browser is designed with a focus on **privacy, security, customization, and a cleaner browsing experience**. Our goal is to reduce unnecessary tracking and provide users with more control over their browsing experience.

Corvixa Browser is one of the first major projects developed by Corvixa Labs, and we look forward to sharing its development with the community.

## Planned Features

* 🛡️ **Tracker protection** — Reduce unwanted tracking across the web
* 🚫 **Ad blocking** — Block intrusive and unnecessary advertisements
* 🔒 **Privacy & security features** — Additional protections designed to make browsing safer
* 🎨 **Extensive customization** — Customize the browser to your preferences
* 🔐 **Password manager** — Securely store and manage passwords
* 🍪 **Cookie protection** — Greater control over cookies and site data
* 🔗 **HTTPS upgrades** — Prefer secure HTTPS connections where available
* 🕵️ **Private browsing** — Browse without keeping normal local browsing history
* ⚙️ **Custom browser settings** — More control over privacy and browser behavior

> **Note:** Corvixa Browser is currently in active development. Features listed above are planned features and may change during development.
